Use case: a character PART SHEET for cut-out rig animation in a top-down 3/4 survivors-like game set inside a cyberpunk datacenter.
Image 1 is the style reference only (line, shading, palette, light); do not copy its subjects.
Image 2 is the ORIGINAL DESIGN of this miniboss from the game this project continues: keep its outer hexagon shape and colour scheme so players recognise it.
Image 3 is a sibling enemy already in the game (the aggregation bug): match its line weight, proportions, limb style and level of detail, but make this bug clearly its own creature.
Subject: the logistics bug: a dark slate hexagon body with a thick sandy-yellow border (like Image 2), two short tank treads on its lower sides, a small route-arrow fin on top and two short antennae; heavy and stubborn.
The game prints the word BUG onto the body itself: put two angry cartoon eyes near the TOP edge of the face and leave the middle of the face as one plain flat area in the frame's inner colour, no letters.
Draw the SAME character split into separate parts, front 3/4 view, all on one canvas:
- top row, centred: the BODY alone, with NO legs and NO arms, facing the viewer;
- bottom row, left to right, each part drawn straight and vertical, complete:
  1) ONE LEG: a single straight stubby leg (thigh and shin in one piece, a slight knee crease), hip end at the top, ankle at the bottom, NO foot, colour dark slate;
  2) ONE FOOT: a single chunky cartoon shoe seen from the side, toe pointing right, colour sandy yellow;
  3) ONE ARM: a single straight arm hanging down, shoulder end at the top, a round mitten hand at the bottom, colour dark slate with a sandy yellow hand.
Composition: every part separate with very wide empty gaps (at least 80 px), nothing touching or overlapping, nothing cut by the canvas edge.
Constraints: no text, no letters, no logos, no numbers, no ground, no shadow, no glow.
