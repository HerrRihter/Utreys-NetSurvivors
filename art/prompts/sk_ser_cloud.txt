Use case: a SKIN for the hero of a top-down 3/4 survivors-like game set inside a dark cyberpunk datacenter: a character PART SHEET for cut-out rig animation.
Image 1 is the hero Сериализация (the base design and the exact parts layout): keep its silhouette, proportions and the U body, the two cubes with their small windows, the eyes, the limb design, the code panel with square marks on its front and the small scanner on each side, so the skin is clearly the same character and fits the same rig.
Skin: ОБЛАКО — a calm, soft variant: smooth soft rounded surfaces without hard seams, a matte pale blue-grey (#a9b8cc) body with soft sky blue (#a6d4ff) and cloud white (#f0f4f8) cubes, relaxed half-closed calm eyes with a gentle smile, thin soft glowing seam lines; soft rounded limbs in the same pale blue-grey (#a9b8cc), white shoes with a soft sky blue (#a6d4ff) sole; the code panel soft and rounded with gently glowing sky-blue marks.
Draw the character split into separate parts, front 3/4 view, all on one canvas:
- top row, centred: the BODY alone, with NO legs and NO arms (the hip and shoulder areas closed and complete), facing the viewer;
- bottom row, left to right, each part straight and vertical, complete: ONE LEG (hip at the top, ankle at the bottom, NO foot), ONE SHOE (side view, toe pointing right), ONE ARM (shoulder at the top, a mitten hand at the bottom).
Every part separate with very wide empty gaps (at least 80 px), nothing touching, overlapping or cut by the canvas edge.
Line and finish: crisp clean vector-like linework, one even thin dark navy outline (#0b1622) around every shape, smooth flat cel shading with one soft shadow tone and one highlight tone, NO grain, NO paint texture, NO noise, NO wobbly or blobby edges.
Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos other than the body shape itself, no ground, no shadow under the parts, no glow, transparent background.
