Use case: a character PART SHEET for cut-out rig animation in a top-down 3/4 survivors-like game set inside a cyberpunk datacenter.
Image 1 is the style reference only (line, shading, palette, light); do not copy its subjects.
Image 2 is the ORIGINAL DESIGN of this enemy from the game this project continues: match its outer shape, frame and colour scheme closely so players recognise it.
Image 3 is a sibling enemy already in the game: match its line weight, proportions and limb style, but make this one clearly its own character.
Subject: the Log Query: a living terminal window: a dark violet-framed rectangular console window with a dark screen, a big magnifying glass held up at its lower right corner (like Image 2), slow and heavily plated.
Put two narrow cartoon eyes on the upper part of the screen; the rest of the screen stays plain dark, no text lines.
Draw the SAME character split into separate parts, front 3/4 view, all on one canvas:
- top row, centred: the BODY alone, with NO legs and NO arms (closed and complete where limbs attach), facing the viewer;
- bottom row, left to right, each part drawn straight and vertical, complete:
  1) ONE LEG: a single straight stubby leg (thigh and shin in one piece, a slight knee crease), hip end at the top, ankle at the bottom, NO foot, colour dark violet;
  2) ONE FOOT: a single chunky cartoon shoe seen from the side, toe pointing right, colour violet;
  3) ONE ARM: a single straight arm hanging down, shoulder end at the top, a round mitten hand at the bottom, colour dark violet with a violet hand.
Composition: every part separate with very wide empty gaps (at least 80 px), nothing touching or overlapping, nothing cut by the canvas edge.
Constraints: no text, no letters, no logos, no numbers, no ground, no shadow, no glow.
